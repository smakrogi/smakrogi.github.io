About

I am a researcher working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, machine learning, and medical image analysis. My work focuses on developing computational methods for extracting clinically meaningful information from complex imaging data and helping support decision-making in biomedical applications.

My interests include computer vision, image processing, pattern recognition, and the application of learning-based methods to biological and medical data. I am particularly interested in building robust algorithms for segmentation, tracking, quantification, and automated interpretation of image and signal data.

I have also gained experience in developing and leading research programs in computational imaging and applied machine learning, while contributing to teaching activities that mentor students and promote interdisciplinary scientific inquiry across engineering, computer science, and biomedical applications.

Research

  • AI and machine learning techniques for clinical decision support
  • Cell segmentation and tracking in time-lapse microscopy sequences
  • Tissue identification, quantification, and characterization
  • Image analysis and machine learning for metabolic disorders
  • Natural disaster mapping in satellite imagery

Selected Projects

  • Biomedical Image Analysis: Developing computational tools for understanding tissue structure and cellular behavior from microscopy and clinical imaging datasets.
  • Clinical Decision Support: Designing AI-driven methods to assist with automated interpretation and decision support in medical applications.
  • Remote Sensing: Applying image-analysis methods to map environmental and disaster-related changes from satellite imagery.
  • Computational Biology: Using segmentation and tracking pipelines to study dynamic biological processes over time.
